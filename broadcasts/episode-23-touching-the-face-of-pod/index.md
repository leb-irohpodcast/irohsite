<p>This week’s broadcast comes to you from 94.4 KPRK, where Justin and Geno have finally slipped the bondage of editorial control and established an anarcho-syndicalist commune. Meanwhile, Caleb has gone into hiding in Brazil following a deeply questionable electoral name-change scheme, leaving the remaining hosts to take turns acting as a sort of executive officer of the week.</p>
<p>Fleet maintenance: the Expedition (FOUR SIX MOD MOTOR BABY) continues leaking fluids, the Stagea gets fresh brake fluid and a Flex Tape intervention, and Geno prepares for crunchy rain season. The Juice also explores the entertainment potential of vintage JDM navigation screens, including the important achievement of driving a Stagea inside a Stagea.</p>
<p>Then the executive committee gets down to business: reviving America’s discontinued automotive nameplates. There are cheap sporty coupes, a quad-turbo V12 Lincoln Zephyr, a Corvair made from a backwards Volt, and an Eldorado large enough to obstruct international shipping. Oldsmobile somehow ends up on the moon, and Dodge presents the toughest philosophical question of the evening: Neon or Viper?</p>
<p>The committee’s findings are clear: giant coupes, tiny coupes, and wagons for everything in between.</p>
